# Reference tables — Dilts Neurological Levels & Spiral Dynamics (v2–v8)

Standing rubric for AI Case Formulation. Every future formulation (mockup and, later,
the real `categorize-session` Edge Function) should phrase its Dilts and Spiral
Dynamics findings in this **Old pattern → New alignment** / **Stuck → Shift** shape,
each closing with a short embodied one-liner ("Immediate words" / "Embodiment words").
Only fill in a level if the raw notes actually support it — an unworked level stays
**Not Applicable**, never a fabricated Old/New pair.

## Table 1 — Robert Dilts' Neurological Alignment

| Level | Old pattern | New alignment | Immediate words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Environment | I need the right people, place, attention, or recognition to feel significant. | I can participate meaningfully in the environment that is here. | I meet what is here. |
| Behavior | I must impress, achieve, display, explain, or remain visible. | I choose the action that is useful, honest, caring, and sufficient. | I do what serves, not what proves. |
| Capabilities | I must know more or perform better to remain valuable. | My abilities are tools for learning, solving, relating, and contributing. | I use my ability; I do not prove myself through it. |
| Beliefs and Values | Being exceptional keeps me respected, safe, and included. | I value presence, integrity, care, growth, connection, freedom, and responsibility. | My values guide me; approval does not define me. |
| Identity | I am the intelligent, beautiful, successful, spiritual, or unique one. | I am a living person who can experience, choose, create, relate, learn, and rest. | I am the participant, not the image. |
| Purpose | My purpose is to become special, higher, or unforgettable. | My purpose is to participate consciously and contribute through what is available now. | I allow life to move through me usefully. |

Note: this reference keeps Beliefs and Values as one combined row (the traditional
6-level Dilts model). The live product currently splits them into two separate rows
(7 levels total) per an earlier decision in this project, so that a values conflict
doesn't get flattened into "belief." Applying this table's Old/New/Immediate-words
*structure* to both rows separately, rather than re-merging them, unless told otherwise.

## Table 2 — V2–V8 Conscious Shift

| Value level | Stuck pattern | Need | Conscious shift | Embodiment words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V2 – Belonging | Will people include, remember, approve of, or care for me? | Belonging and emotional safety. | I can belong without constantly being noticed. | I am here, even without being reflected. |
| V3 – Power | I must stand out, win, dominate, or make them respect me. | Strength, visibility, and protection from helplessness. | I do not need to rise above others to remain powerful. | Strength can be quiet. |
| V4 – Order | I must be correct, disciplined, proper, or spiritually right. | Certainty, structure, and protection from error. | Structure supports my life; it does not determine my worth. | I choose responsible, not perfect. |
| V5 – Achievement | I must achieve, know, earn, look, or perform more than others. | Progress, competence, status, and recognition. | Achievement is something I create; it is not who I am. | What useful result matters here? |
| V6 – Connection | Everyone must understand, appreciate, validate, or include me. | Equality, connection, and emotional recognition. | I can stay connected even when I am not fully understood. | I connect without needing complete validation. |
| V7 – Integration | I understand more patterns or systems, so I am more evolved. | Flexibility, understanding, and effective functioning. | Understanding complexity does not place me above anyone. | What response fits this moment? |
| V8 – Wholeness | I must remain spiritual, expanded, peaceful, or in a higher state. | Unity, wholeness, and transcendence. | Wholeness includes my ordinary, reactive, and limited humanity. | This breath and this task also belong to wholeness. |

Note: these are **functional names** (Belonging, Power, Order, Achievement,
Connection, Integration, Wholeness), not the Purple/Red/Blue/Orange/Green/Yellow/
Turquoise color names used in the first design-review pass. Use the functional
names going forward — they're more directly usable in a coaching conversation than
color codes.

## Display convention

Two columns, more rows — not one dense cell per level. Each level gets its own
header row, then one row per field (Old / New / Immediate words, or Stuck / Need /
Shift / Embodiment words), so each idea is easy to scan on its own line.
